# Image provenance

The original logo is vector artwork extracted directly from `howa-physical-therapy-logo-v2.pdf`. Its colours, paths and lettering are unchanged; only the empty page margins were removed.

Three illustrative photos were generated using the built-in image generation tool. They depict fictional people and are not portraits of HOWA staff or patients. WebP versions are locally optimised encodings. All are stored in `images/`.

## Prompts

- `home-physiotherapy.webp`: Premium natural editorial landscape 3:2 photo of a Pakistani male physiotherapist in blue scrubs guiding a middle-aged Pakistani man through a seated knee extension assessment in a bright Lahore living room. Blue sofa, ivory walls, sheer curtains, soft daylight, realistic anatomy, no logos or text.
- `shoulder-care.webp`: Premium editorial landscape 3:2 photo of a female Pakistani physiotherapist in modest blue scrubs and headscarf guiding a woman in a cream tunic through a gentle seated shoulder mobility assessment at home. Soft natural light, pale blue accents, realistic hands, no text or logos.
- `elderly-care.webp`: Premium editorial landscape 3:2 photo of an elderly Pakistani man in off-white shalwar kameez practising walking with a walking frame and a therapist in navy scrubs attentively at his side. Bright home, ivory walls and blue curtains, realistic anatomy, no text or logos.

`logo.png` is a raster copy of the logo; the site itself uses `logo.svg`.
