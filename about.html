HOWA Physical Therapy Services provides home physiotherapy in Lahore. Our aim is to make personalised care easier to access, especially when travel, discomfort or limited mobility makes appointments difficult.

We take time to understand what you want to return to—moving around your home, enjoying a walk, or feeling more confident in daily activities. Your assessment and care plan start there.

We currently offer home visits only. Our contact location is 10th St, Block L, Khayaban Ameen, Lahore; please call to confirm coverage in your area.
