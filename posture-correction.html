PERSONALISED SUPPORT

Start with understanding.
Build a plan around you.

Your first visit begins with a conversation about your concerns, daily activities and goals. We assess your movement and discuss which approach may suit you. Treatment is adapted to your ability and reviewed as your needs change.

Concerns we can help assess

Postural discomfortDesk-related stiffnessCore weaknessMovement limitations

What your care may include

  • Postural and movement assessment
  • Corrective exercises
  • Core and spinal strengthening
  • Workplace and daily routine guidance

Progress at your own pace

Recovery varies from person to person. We focus on practical goals, manageable exercises and regular reassessment. Where needed, we may recommend a medical review or coordinate care with your treating clinician.
