Privacy & appointments

What we collect

The visit form prepares a WhatsApp message using your name, mobile number, area, selected service and preferred appointment date and time. These details stay in your browser until you choose to open WhatsApp. Press Send in WhatsApp to share the message with HOWA.

How requests are handled

This static website has no appointment database or team dashboard and does not save form entries. Requests sent through WhatsApp are handled by HOWA there. Please share medical records only through a method agreed directly with your treating professional. A website request does not confirm an appointment.

Contact and deletion requests

Call or message 0311-0332314 to ask about your submitted information, request a correction or request deletion. The team may need to verify your identity.

Cookies and external services

This static site does not set application cookies or use advertising trackers. The hosting provider may process technical access logs, including IP addresses. WhatsApp and Google Maps links open external services with their own privacy policies.

Patient education

Our guides offer general information, not a diagnosis or an individual treatment prescription. Exercise suitability depends on your health and assessment. Illustrative photos may be AI-generated and do not identify HOWA staff or patients.

Urgent care

This website and its appointment form are not emergency services. For urgent symptoms, seek immediate local medical care.
